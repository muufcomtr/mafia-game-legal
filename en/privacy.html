Privacy Policy

Mafia Game · Effective date: November 26, 2026

This policy explains how personal data is handled in the Mafia Game mobile app (the “App”). It is written to meet the transparency requirements of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and comparable laws in other countries.

In short

  • No account and no sign-in. Nothing you enter during a game is sent to our servers.
  • Player names, groups and settings are stored only on your device.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking. The App does not ask for location, camera, microphone or contacts access.
  • Only to verify Premium purchases, RevenueCat processes limited data linked to an anonymous ID.
  • Our website uses Google Analytics cookies only if you accept them (see section 12).

2. What the App does

Mafia Game is a social deduction party game in the style of Mafia and Werewolf, played in the same room on a single phone. The voice narrator plays pre-recorded audio files on your device. There is no account or sign-in, and your game content is never sent to us.

3. Information that stays on your device

  • Player names you enter
  • Saved groups
  • Game settings
  • Language and theme preference

This information is kept in your device's local storage. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and we cannot access it. Deleting the App deletes it.

If iCloud or Google backup is turned on, this information may be included in your operating system's backup. Those backups are stored in your Apple or Google account and are covered by that company's policy.

4. Purchases

Premium is offered as weekly and yearly auto-renewing subscriptions and as a one-time lifetime purchase. All payments go through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Neither the App nor we ever see your card or other payment details.

Data handled by the stores is covered by their own policies: Apple Privacy Policy and Google Privacy Policy.

5. RevenueCat

We use RevenueCat, Inc. to verify purchases and keep track of subscription status. For this, RevenueCat processes:

  • An anonymous app user ID, generated at random, which does not contain your name or email address
  • Purchase history and transactions: the product bought, dates, and renewal and cancellation status
  • Basic device and app information: operating system version, app version, and country or store region

We use this data to verify purchases, unlock Premium, keep subscription status up to date and restore purchases. The legal basis is the performance of our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). RevenueCat acts as our processor.

Learn more: RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

6. What we don't do

  • The App shows no ads and does not use your advertising identifier.
  • The App contains no analytics or tracking SDKs, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • The App does not ask for permissions such as location, camera, microphone or contacts.
  • We do not sell your personal data or share it for advertising.

7. Children

Mafia Game is a party game suitable for all ages, including at school and at home. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Player names never leave the device. Because purchases are made through a store account, parents can require approval for purchases using Apple's and Google's parental controls.

8. International transfers

RevenueCat is based in the United States, so the data listed in section 5 may be processed outside your country. Where required, these transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

9. How long data is kept

  • On-device information: until you delete it or remove the App.
  • RevenueCat data: for as long as needed to provide Premium and meet legal obligations, after which it is deleted or anonymized.

10. Security

Apart from the purchase processing described here, the App sends no data. Communication with RevenueCat uses an encrypted connection (HTTPS).

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data and get a copy of it
  • Have inaccurate data corrected
  • Have your data deleted
  • Restrict or object to processing
  • Receive your data in a portable format
  • Complain to your local data protection authority

How to use them

  • On-device information: delete the App to erase all of it. We do not hold this information, so there is nothing to request from us.
  • RevenueCat data: email info@muuf.com.tr. To find your record we may ask for something like the order number on your store receipt. We reply free of charge within one month.

The same rights and the same contact apply under Türkiye's KVKK, Brazil's LGPD, Saudi Arabia's Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) and the UAE's Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021.

12. Our website

The game's website, mafiagame.muuf.com.tr, uses Google Analytics 4, provided by Google Ireland Limited, to measure visits. This section applies only to the website. The App itself contains no analytics.

  • If you accept cookies: Google Analytics stores cookies (names starting with _ga) in your browser and collects the pages you view, the buttons you tap (store links, play, listen to the narrator, card flip), device and browser type, language and approximate location (country and city). The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).
  • If you decline or make no choice: no cookies are stored. Google receives signals without cookies or any lasting identifier, used only for aggregated statistics. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in understanding how the site is used (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Advertising cookies and ad personalisation are always off. Google Analytics does not log or store IP addresses.
  • Event data (pages viewed and buttons tapped) is kept for 2 months; data linked to the cookie identifier is kept for up to 14 months. Google may process it in the United States; these transfers rely on safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.
  • You can change your choice at any time with the “Cookie settings” link at the bottom of every page of the website.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy.

13. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always published on this page with a new effective date. If we make a significant change to how data is processed, we will announce it on this page before it takes effect.
